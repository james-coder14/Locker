Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What Locker is
Locker is a classroom social app for students to connect with classmates, post, message, and organize school life.

What we collect
Account info you provide (name, username, bio, status, photos, email), classroom membership, posts, messages, mates, and basic activity (like last seen for online status).

How it's used
To run the app: show your profile, deliver messages, display classroom feeds, and enforce safety (blocks, reports, mods).

Who can see what
Classmates in shared classrooms can see posts and your profile basics. Mates and people you message can see more. Mods may remove harmful content or profiles.

Messages
Direct and group messages are private to participants. Do not share passwords or private school info.

Your controls
Edit or remove posts/comments you made, block users, remove Mates, and sign out anytime. Contact a mod if you need help deleting an account.

Safety
Report abuse. Do not use Locker for bullying, harassment, or illegal activity. Mods may remove content or accounts that break these rules.

Data storage
Data is stored in Firebase (Google). Keep your login secure.

This is a school project app. Questions? Ask the app owner (James).
