Privacy & Terms

Last updated: October 2026

What Locker is
Locker is a school social app for students to connect with classmates — posts, mates, messages, group chats, polls, and classroom life.

What we collect
Info you provide (name, username, bio, status, photos, email), classrooms you join, posts, comments, polls, messages, mates, anonymous notes metadata, and activity signals (e.g. last seen for online status).

How it's used
To run Locker: profiles, feeds, messaging, invites, and safety (blocks, reports, mutes, mod tools).

Who can see what
Classmates in shared classrooms can see posts and profile basics. Mates lists are public on profiles. Direct messages and group chats are only for participants. Mods can review reported content and, when needed, chats for safety.

Anonymous notes
Notes hide your name from the recipient. Mods may still access sender info to stop abuse. Don't use notes to harass anyone.

Messages & groups
Don't share passwords, private addresses, or anything illegal. Group chats can be left or deleted by creators/mods.

Moderation
Actions like mutes, warnings, and removals may appear from The Locker Staff (not individual names). Mods can mute, lock feeds, remove content, and delete harmful accounts.

Your controls
Edit profile, block users, remove Mates, leave groups, delete your posts/comments, and sign out. Ask Staff if you need account help.

Rules of the community
No bullying, hate, threats, sexual content involving minors, harassment, or spam. No pretending to be staff. Break the rules and you can be muted, locked out of a class, or removed.

Age & school use
Locker is meant for students in a school context. Use common sense offline and online.

Data storage
Hosted on Firebase (Google). Protect your login. We don't sell your data.

Changes
Terms may update as Locker grows. Keep using the app means you accept the current version.
